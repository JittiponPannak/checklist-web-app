# Surface Brief: Staff Mobile Checklist Flow

## Scope & Mode
- Target: `src/components/staff/ChecklistPage.tsx`
- Mode: Operate
- Audience: Branch Floor Staff (Cashier, Stock, Assistant Manager) in retail fresh-mart stores in Thailand

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A high-velocity mobile floor tool designed for one-handed aisle walk-throughs, refusing desktop table conversions and unreachable header buttons in favor of an ergonomic bottom-docked action thumb zone and tactile list rows.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm eggshell ground (`#fffdf9`), rich roast brown typography (`#2b1413`), yolk amber progress glows (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), and tactile card pills with 12px pill radii and crisp 1px borders.

### STORY
The floor staff immediately sees their active branch, shift, and time progress, taps through time-windowed store duties with satisfying visual feedback, and completes shift handovers effortlessly with sticky thumb actions.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Top compact identity pill with live shift timer and streak badge; sticky sub-header with progress ring and category filter tabs; fluid list of large tactile checklist cards (min 56px height); bottom sticky action dock floating over the footer with primary shift completion actions.

### FORM
Tactile floor operational checklist with floating thumb action dock. Seed key: 9ea28233.
