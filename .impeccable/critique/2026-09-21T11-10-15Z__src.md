1. **Integrated Top Bar Utility Ecosystem**:
   - `ThemeToggle`, `NotificationCenter`, and profile controls are consistently integrated into the top app bar across all pages. The floating button that previously occluded mobile submit docks has been eliminated.
2. **Distilled Operational Workflows**:
   - Shift select, position select, and checklist inspection follow a frictionless linear hierarchy with clear progressive disclosure.
3. **Dual-View Responsive Excellence**:
   - High-density management tables automatically adapt into ergonomic mobile cards on smartphone screens without horizontal scroll.
4. **Resilient Error & Feedback Architecture**:
