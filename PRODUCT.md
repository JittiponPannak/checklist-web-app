# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Branch Floor Staff**: Cashiers, stock workers, and assistant branch managers who execute daily shift opening/closing checklists, register float audits, and refrigerator inspections on mobile devices.
- **Store & Executive Managers**: Store managers, general managers, and board/committee members reviewing shift approvals, task completion rates, and exception reports across branches.
- **Central System Admins**: System administrators managing branches, accounts, master checklist definitions, and assignments.

## Product Purpose
Digitize and enforce operational standard operating procedures (SOPs) for retail fresh-mart branches, eliminating paper checklists and ensuring strict accountability for food safety (refrigerator temperatures), cash control (register floats), and shift handoffs.

## Positioning
Tailored directly for Thai retail convenience and fresh-mart operations—built around Thai shift structures (กะเช้า, กะบ่าย, กะควบ), supervisor co-approvals, and dual-role handovers.

## Operating Context
- Fast-paced store aisles, checkout counters, and cold storage rooms.
- High mobile/handheld device usage, frequently operated one-handed while walking or inspecting stock.
- Fluorescent store lighting and variable device screen brightness.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Mandatory 100% completion requirement before closing a shift session.
- Seamless continuation flow between morning and afternoon shifts (ต่อกะ).
- Real-time and background synchronization with Supabase and encrypted client storage.
- Real-time notification updates for managerial approvals and task alerts.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: Eater Egg Fresh Mart (ระบบบันทึกและตรวจสอบเช็คลิสต์การปฏิบัติงาน).
- **Identity**: Farm-fresh, warm, approachable yet rigorously professional.
- **Palette**: Warm eggshell ground (`#fffdf9`), amber yolk accent (`#f59e0b`), deep roast chestnut (`#78483b`), rich brown (`#2b1413`).
- **Typography**: Prompt (modern, warm, optimized for bilingual Thai & Latin clarity).

## Product Principles
1. **Floor-First Usability**: Prioritize one-thumb tap ergonomics and high readability for staff in motion.
2. **Zero Ambiguity Feedback**: Every action (completion, lock, handover, error) must give instantaneous, unmistakable visual confirmation.
3. **Accountability Without Friction**: Strict operational rules (e.g. 100% completion before close) presented with clear scaffolding rather than punitive dead-ends.
4. **Resilient Data Capture**: Protect staff work against connection drops or app switching.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict compliance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA standards (4.5:1 text contrast minimum).
- Clear screen reader support (`aria-live="polite"` progress updates, explicit form labels).
- Full keyboard and focus trapping on all dialogs and modals.
